# P1.6 — Expenses and Monthly / Quarterly Financial Overview

**Branch:** `payment-module-finalization`  
**Live migration:** `20261012031132 / expense_occurrence_recording`  
**Frontend route:** `/expenses`

## Scope

P1.6 extends the existing Expenses module. It does not rebuild the module or change the fee-payment ledger.

- Monthly and quarterly overview of expected recurring costs versus actual recorded expenses.
- Academy Owner manages expenses and reminders for their own academy.
- Super Admin can view across academies but cannot create, edit or delete.
- Recurring reminders are expectations only; they do not generate expenses or imply a payment automatically.
- Each recurring occurrence is recorded manually after the cost has actually been paid.
- Expense records and reminders remain separate from player fee dues and the payment collection ledger.

## Overview calculations

The overview switch displays the current calendar month or current calendar quarter.

- **Monthly expected:** sum of active monthly reminder amounts plus active quarterly reminders that are due by the end of the month or have an expense linked to them in the month.
- **Quarterly expected:** three monthly cycles for each active monthly reminder plus active quarterly reminders due by quarter end or linked to an expense in the quarter.
- **Income collected:** sum of existing `payments` ledger rows with `payment_entry_type = 'payment'` in the selected period, scoped through the player's academy. Correction/adjustment entries are not treated as new cash income.
- **Actual paid:** sum of all recorded expense amounts in the selected calendar period, including one-off expenses.
- **Difference:** expected recurring costs minus actual recorded spend. A negative value means recorded spend exceeds the recurring estimate.
- **Net cash movement:** income collected minus recorded expenses. This is a period flow, not a current cash-in-hand or bank balance.
- The overview queries use period boundaries independently from the register's date/category filters.
- Quarterly reminder due dates earlier than the selected period end are treated as outstanding expectations until manually recorded. These figures are operational estimates, not a ledger balance.

## Manual recurring occurrence workflow

1. On the Recurring reminders tab, the Academy Owner chooses **Record occurrence** for an active reminder.
2. The expense form is prefilled with the reminder category/title and expected amount. The owner confirms the actual expense date and actual amount paid, which may differ from the expected amount.
3. Saving calls `public.record_expense_occurrence(...)`.
4. The RPC verifies the authenticated Academy Owner and current academy, locks the active reminder, inserts one expense linked to that reminder, and advances the reminder's next due date by one month or three months.
5. Expense insert and due-date advance occur in one database transaction. If either operation fails, neither change is committed.
6. An existing expense cannot be attached to or reassigned to a reminder during editing; a new occurrence must use the RPC.

The RPC is SECURITY DEFINER with an empty search path, denies anonymous/PUBLIC execution, and grants execute to authenticated users. The direct client INSERT policy now permits only expenses without a reminder link, forcing linked occurrences through the atomic RPC. RLS remains the academy-scope boundary.

## Validation checklist

- [ ] Academy Owner can create/edit/delete their own expenses and reminders.
- [ ] Super Admin can view but has no mutation controls; direct mutation is denied by RLS.
- [ ] Coach and Parent cannot access the Expenses route or data.
- [ ] Monthly expected totals include active monthly reminders and due/recorded quarterly reminders.
- [ ] Quarterly expected totals count three monthly cycles and quarterly reminders due/recorded in the quarter.
- [ ] Income collected uses existing payment ledger entries in the period, excludes adjustment entries, and respects academy scope.
- [ ] Net cash movement equals collected income minus recorded expenses; it is not labelled as cash-in-hand.
- [ ] Actual totals include only saved expenses inside the period and remain independent of register filters.
- [ ] Record occurrence inserts one linked expense using the actual amount and advances next due date by one frequency interval.
- [ ] An inactive reminder cannot be recorded.
- [ ] Direct client INSERT with a reminder link and direct relinking of an existing expense are rejected.
- [ ] Non-recurring expense create/edit/delete still works.
- [ ] Failure in the occurrence RPC rolls back both expense insert and due-date change.
- [ ] Existing payment collections, fee dues, receipts and parent UPI claim workflow remain unchanged.
