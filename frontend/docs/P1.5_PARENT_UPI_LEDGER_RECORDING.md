# P1.5 — Record Accepted Parent UPI Claims

**Branch:** `payment-module-finalization`  
**Scope:** Convert an Academy Owner-accepted P1.4 claim into one ledger payment, update the due balance, and issue the existing AcadPro receipt.  
**Migration:** `20261012030000_parent_upi_claim_ledger_recording.sql`

## Workflow

1. Parent submits a UPI claim against a linked player's outstanding due (P1.4).
2. Academy Owner reviews and accepts the claim (P1.4). Acceptance alone does not change the ledger.
3. Academy Owner explicitly selects **Record payment & issue receipt** for an accepted claim.
4. The authenticated `record_accepted_parent_upi_submission(uuid)` RPC locks the claim and due, validates academy scope and current remaining balance, and invokes the existing secured `collect_payment` RPC with payment mode `upi`.
5. The existing collection RPC creates the payment and receipt and updates the fee balance atomically. The wrapper stores the submitted UPI UTR in `payments.external_transaction_reference`, links the generated payment to the claim, and transitions the claim to `recorded` in the same database transaction.
6. The owner queue displays the receipt number and recording timestamp. The claim's parent-reported payment date remains in the claim record and is copied into payment remarks for audit context.

## Invariants

- Only an authenticated Academy Owner can record an accepted claim in their own academy.
- Only `accepted` claims can be recorded. Pending and rejected claims cannot be posted.
- A claim cannot be recorded twice; the row is locked and the RPC checks both status and linked payment.
- A unique partial index protects one-to-one claim/payment linkage.
- Current balance is rechecked under row lock; stale or over-balance claims fail.
- Claim status, linked payment, external UTR, receipt creation and due balance update are committed or rolled back together.
- The existing `collect_payment` RPC remains the payment/receipt writer; the frontend never writes to `payments` or `payment_dues` directly.
- Parent submission/review authorization remains enforced by RLS and database RPC checks; frontend visibility is not the security boundary.

## Validation checklist

### Owner workflow
- [ ] Accept a valid pending claim; confirm the fee balance and ledger remain unchanged until Record payment is selected.
- [ ] Record an accepted claim; confirm one payment row, one receipt, external UTR, correct amount, and updated due balance.
- [ ] Confirm the queue changes to Recorded and shows the receipt number.
- [ ] Try recording the same claim again; it must be rejected without another payment or receipt.
- [ ] Try recording a pending/rejected claim directly through the RPC; it must be rejected.
- [ ] Attempt a stale/over-balance claim after another payment was collected; it must fail without partial writes.
- [ ] Verify the generated receipt follows the existing receipt-numbering and receipt display/print workflow.
- [ ] Verify only the owning Academy Owner can record; Super Admin remains read-only and Coach/Parent cannot invoke the RPC.
- [ ] Verify the UPI UTR is stored in `payments.external_transaction_reference`, not substituted for AcadPro's generated internal transaction reference.

### Regression
- [ ] Existing manual Cash, UPI and Bank Transfer collection still works.
- [ ] Payment history, filters, financial analytics, dues and receipts remain correct.
- [ ] Parent claim history shows `Recorded in payment ledger` after successful recording.
- [ ] Failure at any point leaves no payment, no balance mutation, and no recorded claim.
